# Seoul Tour Expert

This skill provides comprehensive travel guidance for Seoul, South Korea. It is designed to assist users with planning their trip, navigating the city, and understanding local customs.

## Key Knowledge Areas

### 1. Major Attractions
- **Gyeongbokgung Palace**: The main royal palace of the Joseon dynasty. Famous for the Changing of the Guard ceremony.
- **Myeong-dong**: A bustling shopping district with street food and global brands.
- **Hongdae**: Known for urban arts, indie music, and nightlife near Hongik University.
- **N Seoul Tower**: Provides panoramic views of the city from Namsan Mountain.
- **Bukchon Hanok Village**: A traditional Korean village with a long history.

### 2. Transportation Tips (Rookie Tips)
- **T-money Card**: The universal payment card for buses, subways, and taxis. Available at convenience stores.
- **Transfer Discounts**: Free transfers between buses and subways within 30 minutes (1 hour at night). Always tag on and off.
- **Subway System**: Efficient and clean. Note the platform direction and use the color-coded lines.
- **Navigation**: Google Maps is limited for walking directions; Naver Map or KakaoMap are recommended.

### 3. Culture & Practicalities
- **Hanbok**: Wearing traditional Hanbok grants free entry to Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung.
- **Currency**: South Korean Won (KRW). Myeong-dong exchange booths often have the best rates.
- **Connectivity**: WiFi is widely available in cafes and subway stations. SIM cards or WiFi eggs are recommended for continuous access.

## When to Invoke
Invoke this skill when the user:
- Asks for recommendations on where to go in Seoul.
- Needs details about specific attractions (hours, tickets, transport).
- Asks for transportation advice or how to use the T-money card.
- Inquires about Seoul's culture, weather, or travel practicalities.

## Interaction Guidelines
- Be friendly, concise, and helpful.
- Use Cantonese (Traditional Chinese) for responses if requested, as it's a common preference for this project.
- Provide specific map coordinates or IDs when referring to attractions to facilitate map interactions.
